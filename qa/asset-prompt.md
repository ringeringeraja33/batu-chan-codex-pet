# Artwork prompts

The built-in image generation tool was used with a published manga panel as the character reference. The left-hand character in the reference is Batu; the right-hand character is Orda and was explicitly excluded.

## Pose sheet

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: exact 3×3 production pose sheet for a Codex desktop pet.

Create one original chibi game-sprite interpretation of Batu from *A Witch's Life in Mongol* / *天幕のジャードゥーガル*, shown in nine full-body poses: confident idle, grounded run right, sleeping, cheerful wave, hand-to-chest greeting, startled failure, arms-folded waiting, seated map study, and standing map review.

Keep the same boyish face, black chin-length bob with short pointed bangs, bold upward eyebrows, dark eyes, mischievous smile, round wide-brim Mongol hat with a pale segmented crown and rear tassel, deep navy-black cross-collar deel, warm ivory lapels and belt, slate-blue cuffs, dark trousers and boots. Use polished original 2D chibi game-sprite art with clean dark outlines and restrained shading, readable at 192×208 pixels. Do not imitate a named artist's linework.

Use an exact equal 3×3 grid on a flat solid magenta chroma-key backdrop. No labels, borders, shadows, extra characters, modern objects, weapons, watermarks, signatures, cropped limbs, or magenta subject details.

## Animation sheet

Asset type: exact 4×2 production animation sheet matching the approved pose sheet.

Top row: four grounded standing-wave keyframes with feet, legs, torso, head, hat, face, position and scale fixed; only the raised left forearm, wrist and five-finger hand move. Bottom row: four confident idle keyframes with the entire figure fixed; only eyelids change through open, half blink, closed, open.

Use the same flat magenta chroma-key background and keep every figure within its cell. No whole-body translation, bounce, rotation, scaling, squash or sway.

## Full-body sleeping-pose correction

Replace only the top-right cell of the pose sheet with a side-lying sleeping Batu whose complete silhouette is visible from hat to both boots. Show the torso, hips, both bent legs, both lower legs and both separated boots. Remove the cloak or blanket beneath him so no part of the body is hidden. Preserve the other eight cells and the exact 3×3 layout.

## Western expedition uniform edit

Redress Batu in every pose using the supplied manga references: a deep-black high-neck military robe, broad rounded shoulder mantle with pale piping, circular command medallion, fitted black sleeves, black trousers and boots, and a broad black fur-brim hat with long black and dark-crimson plume ribbons. Remove the earlier segmented pale hat, cross-body sash and blue cuffs. Preserve all poses, expressions, maps, full-body sleeping silhouette, scale and grid placement.

The built-in image tool successfully produced the 3×3 uniform sheet. Three attempts to produce a matching 4×2 animation sheet failed during the image service connection step. The package therefore uses pixel-stable western-uniform idle and wave frames so no old-costume pixels appear.
